# SecureFlow AI — Demo Script

**Target Duration:** 2:45–3:10
**Core Principle:** Use ONE image throughout the entire demo to tell a cohesive product story.

---

## 00:00–00:12 — Opening (12 sec)

**Screen:** Landing page.

**Voice:**
> "Meet SecureFlow AI, a Cloudinary-powered AI media intelligence and processing platform."
> "It turns raw media into intelligent, optimized, and reusable assets through one continuous workflow."

*(Click **Start Analysis / Open Workspace**)*

---

## 00:12–00:27 — Problem (15 sec)

**Screen:** Landing/product section (or transitional scroll down the landing page).

**Voice:**
> "Today's media workflows are fragmented. Uploading, understanding, moderating, cropping, removing backgrounds, optimizing, and managing assets often happens across different tools."
> "SecureFlow AI brings those operations together."

*(Show the visual pipeline: Upload → Analyze → Process → Transform → Optimize → Save)*

---

## 00:27–00:45 — Dashboard (18 sec)

**Screen:** Go to `/dashboard`.

**Voice:**
> "The dashboard provides a command center for media intelligence, processing, asset management, search, and reporting."

*(Briefly show the Sidebar, KPI cards, Workspace, Intelligence, and Processing sections. **Do not click every item.**)*

---

## 00:45–01:00 — Media Workspace (15 sec)

**Screen:** Open `/dashboard/workspace`.

**Voice:**
> "The core product experience is the Media Workspace."
> "This is where a single asset moves through the entire lifecycle."

*(Pause for a second)*

---

## 01:00–01:20 — Upload + AI (20 sec)

**Screen:** Upload your **preselected demo image**.
*(The image MUST have a person/product, background, multiple objects, and a clear focal subject.)*

**Voice:** (While processing)
> "I'll start by uploading one image."
> "The asset is uploaded through Cloudinary, and the workspace can immediately surface its metadata and AI intelligence."

*(Show asset dimensions, format, tags, confidence, moderation, and analysis)*

**Voice:**
> "Instead of treating the asset as a file, SecureFlow AI turns it into structured media intelligence."

---

## 01:20–01:38 — Smart Crop (18 sec)

**Screen:** Open Smart Crop. Change Square → Portrait.

**Voice:**
> "First, I'll apply Smart Crop."
> "The content-aware crop keeps the important visual subject in the composition while adapting the asset to a new aspect ratio."

*(Show before/after)*

---

## 01:38–01:53 — Background Removal (15 sec)

**Screen:** Open Background Removal. Turn it on.

**Voice:**
> "Next, I can isolate the main subject by removing the background."

*(Pause. Show the Before/After: ORIGINAL → BACKGROUND REMOVED)*

---

## 01:53–02:08 — Optimization (15 sec)

**Screen:** Open Optimization.

**Voice:**
> "Once the asset is ready, I can optimize how it is delivered."

*(Show Format, Quality, Auto Format, Auto Quality)*

**Voice:**
> "This lets the same source asset be delivered in an optimized format and quality for the target experience."

---

## 02:08–02:23 — Transform Studio (15 sec)

**Screen:** Open Transform. Apply **ONE** transformation (e.g., Rotate 90° + Grayscale).

**Voice:**
> "The Transform Studio provides additional controls such as rotation, flipping, dimensions, and visual effects."

*(Keep this clean. Do not demonstrate five effects.)*

---

## 02:23–02:40 — Save + Library (17 sec)

**Screen:** Click **Save to Media Library**. Go to Library.

**Voice:**
> "Finally, I save the processed asset to the Media Library."

*(Show the new asset in the library)*

**Voice:**
> "The workflow is now complete."
> "The raw file has become an analyzed, processed, optimized, and reusable media asset."

---

## 02:40–02:55 — Architecture (15 sec)

**Screen:** Show the architecture diagram from the README.

**Voice:**
> "Under the interface, Next.js provides the application layer, Cloudinary provides media infrastructure, AI capabilities and transformations, Supabase provides persistence, and OpenAI supports application-level analysis."

*(Keep it short. Focus on how Cloudinary is used.)*

---

## 02:55–03:05 — Closing (10 sec)

**Screen:** Return to the landing page.

**Voice:**
> "SecureFlow AI brings media intelligence, AI processing, transformation, optimization, and asset management into one workflow."

*(Pause)*

**Voice:**
> "Upload once. Understand the asset. Process it intelligently. Optimize it. Save it."
